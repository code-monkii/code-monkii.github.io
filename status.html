CHAMPIONS OU GUIDE

Status Effects

What each status condition does in battle

MAJOR STATUS CONDITIONS

Burn

  • The Pokémon takes 1/16 of its maximum HP as damage each turn.
  • Physical attack damage is halved.
  • Fire-type Pokémon cannot be burned by Fire-type moves.

Paralysis

  • Speed is reduced to 50%.
  • The Pokémon has a 12.5% (1/8) chance to be unable to move each turn.
  • Electric-type Pokémon cannot become paralyzed by Electric-type moves.

Poison

  • The Pokémon takes 1/8 of its maximum HP as damage each turn.
  • Poison- and Steel-type Pokémon cannot normally be poisoned.

Badly Poisoned

  • The Pokémon takes damage each turn that increases over time.
  • Damage starts at 1/16 of maximum HP, then increases by 1/16 each turn.
  • Damage is capped at 15/16 of maximum HP in the turn counter.
  • Switching out resets the damage counter.

Sleep

  • The Pokémon cannot move while asleep.
  • The first turn asleep is guaranteed; the Pokémon will not attempt to wake on that turn.
  • It will have a 1-in-3 chance of being cured on its second turn
  • The Pokemon will be cured on its third turn.
  • Sleep from the move Rest has no chance of being cured until its third turn.

Frozen

  • The Pokémon cannot move while frozen.
  • It has a 25% chance to thaw before moving each turn.
  • The Pokemon is cured on its third turn after becoming frozen.
  • The Pokemon can thaw out if it uses or takes damage from a Fire-type move or certain other moves.
  • Ice-type Pokémon cannot normally be frozen.

OTHER COMMON STATUS

Confusion

  • Confusion lasts for 1–5 turns.
  • Each turn the Pokémon has a 33.3% chance to hit itself.
  • Self-confusion damage is 40 base power, adjusted for type and other modifiers.

Flinch

  • The Pokémon cannot move during the current turn.
  • Flinching is cleared at the end of the turn.
  • Inner Focus and Steadfast Pokemon cannot be flinched.

Infatuation

  • The Pokémon has a 50% chance to become immobilized while infatuated.
  • It lasts until the opposing Pokémon leaves battle or the infatuated Pokémon is cured.
  • Infatuation only occurs between Pokémon of opposite genders.

Bound or Trapped

  • The Pokémon cannot switch out normally for up to 6 turns.
  • The Pokémon takes damage equal to 1/8 of its maximum HP at the end of each turn.
  • Caused by moves like Bind, Fire Spin, Infestation, Sand Tomb, Snap Trap, Whirlpool, and Wrap.
  • Rapid Spin and Mortal Spin removes the bound status.

Taunted

  • The Pokémon cannot use Status moves.
  • Taunt lasts for 3 turns if the user acts before the target, or four turns if the target acted before the user.

Leech Seeded

  • The Pokémon takes damage equal to 1/16 of its max HP at the end of every turn, and this damage is absorbed by the opposing Pokemon

Salt Cured

  • The Pokémon takes damage equal to 1/16 of its max HP at the end of every turn
  • For Water- and Steel-type Pokémon, they take damage equal to 1/8 of their max HP at the end of every turn

TERRAIN EFFECTS

Grassy Terrain

  • All Pokémon on the ground recover 1/16 of their maximum HP at the end of each turn.
  • Grass-type moves have their power increased by 50%.

Electric Terrain

  • All Pokémon on the ground cannot be put to sleep.
  • Electric-type moves have their power increased by 50%.

Misty Terrain

  • All Pokémon on the ground cannot be affected by status conditions.
  • Dragon-type moves have their power decreased by 50%.

Psychic Terrain

  • All Pokémon on the ground cannot be affected by priority moves.
  • Psychic-type moves have their power increased by 50%.

BATTLE STATUSES AND EFFECTS

Trick Room

  • Speed is reversed for 5 turns.

Tailwind

  • All Pokémon on the ground have their Speed doubled for 5 turns.

Reflect

  • Halves the damage taken from physical moves. In Double Battles, this damage is reduced by 1/3 instead.

Light Screen

  • Halves the damage taken from special moves. In Double Battles, this damage is reduced by 1/3 instead.

Aurora Veil

  • Halves the damage taken from physical and special moves. In Double Battles, this damage is reduced by 1/3 instead.

Stealth Rock

  • Pokémon that switch into battle will take damage equal to 1/8 of their max HP. This damage differs depending on the Pokémon's type matchup with the Rock type.

Spikes

  • Pokémon that switch into battle will take damage equal to 1/8 of their max HP. This damage will increase if 2 layers of Spikes are set, and again if 3 layers are set. Pokémon such as Flying types or ones with the Levitate Ability are unaffected.

Toxic Spikes

  • Pokémon that switch into battle will be poisoned or, if 2 layers of Toxic Spikes are set, badly poisoned. Pokémon such as Flying types or ones with the Levitate Ability are unaffected.
